# 🌾 Catisle *(2D)*

Be a farmer cat in this 2D, cozy, top-down life simulator. ⋆ ⟡🌾⊹⋆˙🐈⟡ ⋆.🪵˚ ⊹₊🪓⟡ ⋆

***Catisle*** currently has: player movement, world interaction/manipulation, item collection, and an explorable map.<br>
Made in GameMaker! :]

## ❣️ Features

**🧭 Exploration**<br>
Explore a small, custom-made map<br>
Find and collect resources<br>

**🌱 Farming**<br>
Deforest your land and collect logs<br>
Kill your beloved cow for milk

**🆓 FREE to Play**<br>
No ads or micro-transactions<br>
Perfect for fans of Stardew Valley<br>

## 🕹️ Controls

Run around the map and explore what I have implemented thus far!

`WASD / arrow keys` → Move<br>
`Mouse Left-click` → Damage highlighted items<br>
